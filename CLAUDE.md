# Topo Finance — Financed Emissions Estimator

## What this project is
A single-file, browser-based estimator that helps treasury professionals estimate their company's **financed emissions** (Scope 3, Category 15) across banking relationships and money market fund holdings. Built as a lead-generation and awareness tool for Topo Finance, ending with an email CTA for deeper engagement.

The tool now uses **real emission factor data** from Topo Finance (per-bank GSIB factors from "Data Analytics (Intensities..) 30.10.2025.xlsx" and MMF/transition risk data from "Topo Finance — Financed Emissions Estimator Data.xlsx").

### Step 1 — Select Global Banks
- **Title:** Which Global Banks do you bank with?
- **Subtitle:** Select all the banks where your company holds current, deposit accounts. You'll enter amounts on the next screen.
- **Accordion (What is a Global Bank?):** G-SIBs are the world's largest banks (29 total). Topo Finance has estimated CO2e intensity for 21 of them + 6 large institutions.

### Step 2 — Bank Deposits
- **Title:** How much do you hold at each bank?
- **Subtitle:** Enter the amount in USD million (exact or rounded), equivalent of cash current account, deposits at each bank. Leave blank or enter USD 1m if you do not want to enter the exact balance. Please choose a similar point in time for all positions (for instance reported year-end).
- **Total label:** Total balances of cash (current account / deposits)

### Step 3 — Money Market Funds
- **Title:** Money Market Fund Holdings (Optional)
- **Subtitle:** Enter the approximate USD equivalent held in money market funds, grouped by the fund's base currency. Leave blank or skip if you don't hold MMFs.
- **Accordion (About this estimate):** Large number of MMF products with varying compositions. Estimate based on standard liquidity funds (85-95% financial institutions). Average of selected funds in EUR/USD/GBP.

### Review
- **Title:** Review of your information
- **Subtitle:** Control the information you entered. Use the Back button to make changes.

### Scope (optional)
- **Title:** Your company CO2e emissions (in tons of CO2e) (Optional)
- **Subtitle:** If your company has publicly reported their Carbon Emissions, you have the option to enter them here. This will be used to compare the estimated financed emissions to your total reported footprint.
- **Tab 1:** Reported CO2e Emissions: Total scope 1, scope 2 and scope 3 aggregated
- **Tab 2:** Reported CO2e Emissions: Enter each scope separately

### Results CTA
- **Title:** Go deeper with Topo Finance, at no cost
- **Body:** Topo Finance is a nonprofit. We offer treasury teams free access to deeper estimations, and practical strategies for engaging your banks on emissions reduction. If you'd like a confidential, tailored analysis or want to understand your options, get in touch. There is no cost.

## UI decisions made

- Persistent header title: "Financed Emissions Estimator" centred in the header bar

- Progress bar sits below the header, fills as user advances through steps
- Step counter format: "Step X of Y" — Y updates dynamically
- Results bar chart is CSS-only, no libraries, bars animate in on load
- Print/PDF button triggers `window.print()` with a clean print stylesheet
- Banks sorted alphabetically (not by country)
